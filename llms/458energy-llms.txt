# 45-8 ENERGY

> 45-8 ENERGY is a French exploration and production company, founded in 2017 and headquartered
> in Metz (with an office in Lyon), specialised in helium and natural (native) hydrogen in
> Europe. It covers the full value chain — geoscientific exploration, production, distribution
> and recycling. It delivered the first helium produced on Western European soil in 2024 and
> operates RECYCL'He, Europe's first mobile helium recovery and recycling unit. In April 2026
> Ad Terra took a majority stake in the company.
